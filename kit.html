The kit

A standard box, a rail it locks onto, and whatever you put inside. Most camp gear sits in a closet for most of the year. This is built to be in use at home first.

Why one standard

Gear you use every week shows its flaws at home, long before it reaches a riverbank. So the kit is made for the mudroom or the garage first, and the trip is the easy part.

Every module is the same size on the outside. A rail, a cart or a shelf built for one box takes them all, and a new module never means a new way to carry it.

Three parts

The box
A light frame with a slide-in tray. Same footprint every time. The contents change; the box does not.
The rail
L-track, the slotted cargo rail used in vans and trailers. The same rail bolts to a garage wall, a mudroom wall or a vehicle floor, and the box locks onto it.
The contents
Wet gear, chairs, rods, and later food, power and cold. Whatever you reach for every week.

What we’re making

Trailhead First, in build
The wet-gear and seating box. It has no fuel, no power and no water in it, which is why it goes first. Chairs and a small table ride along.
Rod cradle First, in design
A printed pair of closed rod holders for the rail. Each rod is held at two closed points, with the reel foot captured. For a garage wall or a cargo bay.
Home dock Next, to be drawn
The box is heavy once it is full. A wall rail or a rolling cart, using the same rail and lock, makes it furniture instead of luggage.
Power, cold, dry food Later, if the first ones earn it
People already use a power station and a 12 volt fridge both at home and in the field. A tray and a plan for each is the next idea. We will not promise one until we have used it ourselves.

Vehicles come second

At home a rail holds a weight. In a vehicle it has to hold that weight through a hard stop, which is a much harder job and different for every vehicle.

So we do the home half first, and the vehicle half one vehicle at a time. Our first test vehicles are a 2006 Cadillac Escalade ESV and a high-roof Sprinter van. We will not call a vehicle ready until we have measured it and tested its anchors.

Hear when there’s something to buy
