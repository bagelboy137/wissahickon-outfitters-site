Two friends
and a printer

Wissahickon Outfitters is Conor and Pete, in Philadelphia. We bought a 3D printer in November 2026 and started making the gear we wanted to buy.

Who we are

Conor draws the modules, and fishes, paddles and rides around the Wissahickon. Pete has a high-roof Sprinter van, and the printer lives at his place next to it.

We are not a factory. We make small batches, one product at a time, and we use each one ourselves before we sell it.

The name

The Wissahickon is the creek and the valley park that run through northwest Philadelphia. It is where we walk, fish, paddle and ride, and it is the first place we test anything.

What we will and won’t make

  • Our own designs only.We do not sell prints of other people’s models.
  • ASA for anything that lives in a vehicle.A parked car reaches 60 to 80 °C. Ordinary PLA softens at about 55 to 60 °C, so it does not ride in a cabin.
  • Printed parts hold things in place.They never are the only thing between a heavy object and a person.
  • No gas appliances.We may build a rack or a cart for a stove you bought. We will not make a burner.
  • Nothing over the seats.No overhead racks in a cabin, ever.
  • “Tested” means we can show you the test.We will not say tested or rated until it is true.
